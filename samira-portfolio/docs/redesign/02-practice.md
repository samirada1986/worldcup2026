# 02 · Artistic practice assessment

Source of truth: the artist's own portfolio document (biography, section texts, photographs). Nothing here is added from outside it, and nothing about exhibitions, awards or intentions is assumed.

## What is verified (her words)

- Iranian visual artist and photographer, based in Tehran; nearly a decade of photography.
- Began with **documentary and street photography**: work concerned, above all, with **the relationship between people and their surroundings, with everyday moments and lived experience**.
- In recent years the practice has moved toward **personal, long-term projects** that start from an individual experience and reach toward a wider question about the human, the body and the experience of living.
- Background in pure mathematics and industrial engineering; works as a senior product manager in digital products. She describes this as giving her work a research-driven, structured approach.

## What the published photographs show (description, not interpretation)

Eight photographs, four black-and-white and four colour, all landscape format (~3 : 2):

| | Black and white | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A man walks toward a painted archway; a second figure stands deep inside the painted tunnel | Three women at a lake's edge; two point at the sky |
| 2 | A lone figure walks beneath a painted mural of trees and a path | Two women at a railing, looking out over a lake |
| 3 | A man carries a bunch of flowers past a swan mural, in dappled light | A lone figure on a heap of concrete tetrapods against a clear sky |
| 4 | A hooded figure passes a wall chalked with equations and a cube | A small blue tent in a red-rock canyon |

**Recurring structure (visible, therefore safe to state):** a single person meets a *surface* or an *expanse*: a painted wall, a chalked wall, a stretch of water, a heap of concrete, a canyon. In the black-and-white half the surface is an *image* (a mural, a diagram); in the colour half it is *space* (water, sky, rock). People are mostly seen from behind or in profile and are small in the frame, or share it with a backdrop that depicts something else.

## Positioning (derived, and what it is not)

**Derived positioning:** contemporary documentary / street photography in which people are shown *against constructed or depicted surfaces and against open ground*, sequenced as a short, quiet series.

**Not claimed:** the "research directions" in the brief (embodied photography, body as subject and medium, performance, installation, identity, self-representation) **are not evidenced by the published series**. Her biography says her recent direction is personal and long-term, but the published work does not yet show it. They stay out of the public copy until she releases that work and writes about it herself. The design keeps room for it (see `04-directions.md`: three layout modes, research/archive layout, per-image notes).

## Constraints the work imposes on the design

1. **Native size ~1110 × 740 px.** The only source is the artist's portfolio document. The photographs must not be enlarged past that; full-bleed hero treatments would visibly soften them.
2. **A short series (8).** Too few for an immersive spatial walk-through; enough for a book-like sequence with a clear first, last and middle.
3. **Two tonal halves** (black-and-white, colour) suggest two "signatures" of a book; the order (artist-controlled) is respected, with pairs chosen by what they share (the two lake photographs; the two solitary figures in landscape).
4. **No titles, dates or captions were supplied.** The site therefore shows plate numbers only and plain-language alt text. Anything more must come from her.
5. **Bilingual from the start.** Persian is a full right-to-left layout, not a mirror.
